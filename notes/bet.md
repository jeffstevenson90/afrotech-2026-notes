## How BET is using AI

- **Operations first:** BET is piloting an AI app to handle seating for a major awards show, a job that used to take days. The speaker was openly "nervous" and planned to review the output personally.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Pilot AI on painful operational tasks with a human reviewer.** High-effort, rules-heavy, easy-to-check work is the right shape for a first use case.
- **Expect AI to miss domain and cultural knowledge.** "AI agreed when corrected" isn't validation.
- **Consistency and composure under pressure build trust** with stakeholders.
