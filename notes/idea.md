## Summary in plain terms

Two early-stage founders asked how to build momentum and revenue:

- **Shana Norwood, Write Notes:** media for children with learning differences; self-funded with three proof-of-concept episodes and early pilot data.
- **Franny, a social club for Black founders:** about 250 members since mid-September, currently free; asked whether to start charging.

The advice: **show, don't tell**, **go where your customers are**, and **charge from day one**.

## Key callouts

- **Proof beats claims.** Investors and partners want real usage data, not just "evidence-based."
- **Turn events into conversion events.** Host in-person sessions, let people see the value, get them to sign up on the spot, and film it for marketing.
- **Get outside.** Digital channels are crowded; show up where customers gather.
- **You don't need a full library to launch.** Commit to a regular release schedule.
- **Charge from day one,** with tiered pricing for different budgets.
- **Say what you need, out loud.** Warm investor introductions come from being visible.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Lead with data, not methodology** when pitching a dashboard, model or AI pilot.
- **Demo live and get commitment in the room.**
- **Ship on a regular schedule** rather than waiting for a complete product.
- **Make the team's value and capacity visible** rather than absorbing every request quietly.
