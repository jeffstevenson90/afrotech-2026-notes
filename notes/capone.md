## Summary in plain terms

Capital One technology leaders discussed what it takes to get real value from AI, not just demos: a modern cloud data foundation, high-quality well-governed data, and human judgment to direct a growing workforce of AI agents. As models become commodities, **data quality is the differentiator**.

## Key callouts

- **2030 vision: "everyone is a CEO of agents."** Agents already review code, open pull requests and gather feedback in minutes. The critical skill becomes directing agents and making sure they do what they're supposed to.
- **Infrastructure comes first.** Capital One was among the first financial services firms to go all-in on the public cloud, which made its machine learning (e.g., fraud detection) possible.
- **Hallucinations are often a data problem.** Models make things up when they lack context, usually because they can't reach the right data. Keep data centralized, well-managed and accessible.
- **Garbage in, garbage out still applies.** Data must be accurate, high-quality and checked for bias, and data ecosystems increasingly need to be **self-healing** in real time.
- **Models are becoming commodities; data is the edge.** Recent gains in reasoning models came from expert-curated domain data.
- **Domain knowledge enriches AI,** on top of trustworthy data, governance and security controls.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Our data foundation is our AI strategy.**
- **Treat poor AI answers as missing context** and check data access first.
- **Move from reactive to proactive data quality:** monitoring, alerting and auto-remediation.
- **Capture domain knowledge:** business definitions, rules and context.
- **Prepare the team to manage agents:** directing, reviewing and validating AI work.
- **Encourage deeper data skills.**
