**Speakers:** U.S. Rep. Ayanna Pressley (D-MA, 7th District), in conversation with a former Google AI researcher · **Note:** policy positions and figures are summarized as the speakers stated them.

## Summary in plain terms

A conversation about who governs AI and who it answers to. Rep. Pressley described her focus on making sure AI doesn't deepen existing inequities. She promoted the **AI Civil Rights Act** (co-led with Rep. Yvette Clarke), which aims to bar biased algorithms in major decisions and require transparency and bias testing. They also discussed data centers' community impact and what accountability for AI harm should look like. Her refrain: **"Don't lose the plot, because the plot is the people."**

## Key callouts

- **AI decisions are civil-rights decisions.** AI already influences loans, public-safety flags and hiring. She cited a 23-year life expectancy gap within three miles of her district.
- **Hiring tools need scrutiny,** given employers' growing use of AI in HR.
- **What the AI Civil Rights Act would do (as described):** prohibit biased algorithms in consequential decisions, require transparency and bias testing, and protect people who come forward about harmful products.
- **Responsible implementation means** disclosure that AI is used, representation, ethics, governance and rigorous testing.
- **Accountability should match the harm,** civil or criminal depending on severity, with repair where harm occurs.
- **Data centers and communities.** "The people closest to the pain should be closest to the power." Communities need a positive model to push for, not just opposition.
- **Heat recapture as a concrete ask:** server heat can warm water for nearby buildings, as in parts of Europe.
- **Other harms flagged:** deepfakes and data privacy.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Regulatory scrutiny of decision algorithms is rising,** and insurance models face similar questions.
- **Build bias testing and explainability in now** for customer-impacting models.
- **Disclose AI use** in decisions and outputs that affect people.
- **Create a safe path to raise concerns** about unfair outcomes.
