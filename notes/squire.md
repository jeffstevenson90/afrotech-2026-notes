**Speaker:** Dave Salvant (confirm), co-founder of Squire, barbershop management software · **Note:** figures are as stated on stage.

## Summary in plain terms

Squire's co-founder shared how he built a product for a community-rooted industry by deeply understanding the customer, to the point of buying and running a barbershop. He spoke candidly about bias he faced fundraising as a Black founder, and described how Squire is adding AI agents to take admin work off shop owners.

## Key callouts

- **Know the customer and the culture.** The first ~5 customers came from founder-led sales. "It's easy to say no the first time. It's hard to say no the seventh time."
- **The market is the only truth.** Persist, but listen when enough customers say an idea isn't viable.
- **Live the problem.** Running a shop showed the hardest part was **people management**: payroll, tips and paying people on time. That became the core product.
- **Think big on market size.** Investors asked if it was "just for Black barbershops"; he framed it for the whole market.
- **Bias in fundraising is real.** One investor asked them to hire non-Black executives. Grow tough skin; you only need one yes.
- **Traction is all that matters now.** Strong retention (he cited about 300% net revenue retention) led to preemptive rounds on clean terms.
- **Stay steady through the lows.** He skipped his salary before the Series A.

## How Squire is using AI

- **An AI phone operator** answers calls, books appointments and refills cancelled slots.
- **Adoption is gradual;** recovered missed revenue is what wins holdouts.
- **Balance automation with humans:** an AI support assistant plus 24/7 human support and success managers.
- **Feedback bias:** unhappy customers are loudest, so seek out satisfied ones too.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Sit with the users** before building a dashboard or AI tool.
- **Prove value with adoption and retention metrics,** not features.
- **Lead AI rollouts with a clear win.**
- **Automate the admin, keep the human touch.**
- **Balance feedback** by asking quiet, satisfied users too.
