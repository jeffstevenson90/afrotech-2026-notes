## Summary in plain terms

GE HealthCare's HR chief and CIO presented together on purpose: AI transformation only works when **technology and people strategy are led as one**. Skills are going out of date faster than training can keep up, roles are merging, and "bolting on" AI wastes most of its value. The real opportunity is **redesigning how work gets done**.

## Why the workforce has to change

- **Skills are expiring faster:** about 40% of core skills will change by 2030 (World Economic Forum).
- **Training alone isn't enough;** learning must be continuous and tied to outcomes.
- **Roles are converging:** a solution architect can now use AI to write the code too.
- **Operating models are shifting** as processes are reworked.

## The three skills that matter most (Adam Holton)

1. **Learning agility and curiosity.**
2. **Decision quality:** AI generates insight faster than we do, so our value moves upstream to framing questions, prioritizing, testing and deciding.
3. **Human connection:** compassion and resilience remain the most important skills.

## What real workforce readiness looks like (Jahid)

- **People first:** skills, mindset and culture, partnered with HR.
- **Plus quality data and redesigned processes,** with business leaders accountable.
- **Don't just add AI to the old process.** If AI gathers the data and builds the deck, ask whether the meeting is needed at all.

## Getting employees to see AI as opportunity

- **Leaders must model it.**
- **Give access and room to experiment** before any big transformation talk.
- **Be transparent** and push back on fear-driven headlines.
- **Involve employees** through communities of practice.
- **Use practical, role-specific examples.**
- **AI leadership can come from anywhere,** not just IT.

## Building a culture of innovation

- **Psychological safety:** don't punish failure.
- **Remove friction:** an idea that needs 15 approvals never starts.
- **Scale what works** across the organization.
- **Reimagine the delivery lifecycle:** GE's IT program is rethinking how projects run, the roles involved and the tools. It's already saving millions.
- **Small improvements compound:** 1% better every day is about 37x in a year.

## How to future-proof your career

- **Unlearn.** "A best practice is simply the way we learned how to do something in yesterday's constraints."
- **Build AI fluency** by using tools continuously.
- **Learn to redesign work,** which he called the most important leadership skill of the future.

## From the Q&A

- **Preserve what matters:** anchor on culture and values, and don't let AI replace thinking and decision-making.
- **What they'd pay outside experts for:** new ways to train people continuously, and people skilled at redesigning work.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Redesign, don't bolt on** our major BI workflows.
- **Shift our value upstream** to framing questions and supporting decisions.
- **Partner with HR and business leaders** on AI change.
- **Model AI use** and give the team safe space to experiment.
- **Prepare for merging roles.**
- **Aim for small daily improvements.**
- **Make continuous learning part of the work.**
