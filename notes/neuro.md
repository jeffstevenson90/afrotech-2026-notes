## Summary in plain terms

A talk on neurodivergence, mainly ADHD, in the workplace and leadership, with a focus on Black professionals. The speaker described how neurodivergent minds work, the hidden cost of "masking," the strengths these minds bring to leadership, and how AI can support executive function (planning and organizing).

## Key callouts

- **How the ADHD brain works at work.** Hyperfocus can produce weeks of work in hours; tasks that are unclear, overwhelming or forced can freeze progress. Time feels abstract.
- **Masking vs. code-switching.** Code-switching adjusts outward things; masking is internal and exhausting, and often rewarded.
- **Labels land differently for Black kids,** which makes many families wary of diagnosis.
- **Strengths for leadership:** fast pattern recognition, big-picture thinking, spotting broken processes, deep empathy, and calm in a crisis.
- **Watch-outs:** perfectionism, imposter syndrome, over-committing, over-apologizing and rejection sensitivity.
- **"Disclosure is context."** Explain what's going on and how you'll handle it, then move on. Whether to disclose at work is personal.
- **Ask before you fix:** "Do you want me to listen, or do you want advice?"

## Using AI as support

- Break big tasks into steps you can finish in under 10 minutes.
- Get realistic time estimates (add about a 25% buffer) and build a daily schedule.
- Summarize meetings and organize information.
- Prompt specifically; AI defaults to neurotypical assumptions.
- Never put confidential company information into unapproved tools.

## Learnings and signals for the team

- **Lead with the "why" and the point.**
- **Make AI planning help normal for everyone,** so no one has to disclose anything to get support.
- **Create safety for disclosure** and know our accommodation process.
- **Give feedback with care;** some teammates feel criticism more intensely.
- **Use the strengths** in incident response, process improvement and root-cause analysis.
